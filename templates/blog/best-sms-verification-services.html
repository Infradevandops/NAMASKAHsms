Vrenum — Best for Reliability & Automatic Refunds

Vrenum provides real US mobile numbers with automatic refunds enforced in code. If no SMS arrives, credits return to your wallet before the session closes — no support ticket needed.

  • ✅ Automatic refunds — no questions asked
  • ✅ Real US mobile numbers (not VoIP)

SMS-Activate — Best for Country Coverage

SMS-Activate has the widest country coverage and lowest prices, but refunds are manual and the interface is more complex. Good for developers who need multi-country support.

TextVerified — Similar to Vrenum

TextVerified is the provider Vrenum is built on top of. Direct access gives you the same numbers at similar pricing. Vrenum adds a cleaner dashboard, Paystack payments (for African users), and a simpler API.

Receive-SMS-Online — Free but Unreliable

Free shared numbers that anyone can see. Not suitable for real verifications — codes are visible to all users and numbers are often already registered with services.

How to Choose

  • Need automatic refunds + clean UX? Vrenum.
  • Need multi-country coverage? SMS-Activate.
