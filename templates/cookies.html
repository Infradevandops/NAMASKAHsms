Cookie Policy

Last updated: November 2026

What Are Cookies?

Cookies are small text files stored on your device when you visit a website. They help the site remember your preferences and understand how you use it.

We keep our cookie use minimal. We do not use advertising or tracking cookies.

Third-Party Services

We use the following third-party services that may set cookies or process data:

  • Cloudflare Turnstile — Bot protection on login and registration forms. Privacy policy
  • Sabilytics — Privacy-first analytics (no personal data, no cross-site tracking). Only loaded with your consent. Privacy policy
  • Paystack — Payment processing. Only active on payment pages. Privacy policy
  • Sentry — Error tracking (no cookie set; uses session context only). Privacy policy

We do not use Google Analytics, Facebook Pixel, or any advertising networks.
