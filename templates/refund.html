Refund Policy

Fair and transparent refund terms

Last Updated: November 2026

Our Commitment: Automatic refunds for failed verifications. No support ticket needed, no questions asked.

1. Automatic Refunds

You automatically receive a full refund if:

1.1 SMS Not Received

  • No SMS arrives within the service timeout period (typically 2–10 minutes)
  • Refund is instant — credited to your wallet balance immediately
  • No action required from you

1.2 Technical Failures

  • Phone number is invalid or blocked by the provider
  • Service provider error on our end
  • API timeout or connection failure

1.3 Refund Timeline

Instant. Automatic refunds are credited to your wallet the moment failure is detected — before the session even closes.

2. Manual Refunds (Cancellations)

2.1 When You Can Cancel

You can cancel a pending verification and receive a full refund if:

  • The SMS has not yet been received
  • Verification status is still "pending"
  • You cancel before the timeout period expires

2.2 How to Cancel

  1. Go to your verification history in the dashboard
  2. Click "Cancel" on the pending verification
  3. Confirm — refund is instant to your wallet

2.3 Refund Amount

100% refund if cancelled before SMS is received.

3. Non-Refundable Scenarios

The following situations are not eligible for refunds:

3.1 Completed Verifications

  • SMS was successfully received and displayed
  • Verification status is "completed"

3.2 User Error

  • Wrong service selected
  • Changed your mind after SMS was received

3.3 Third-Party Rejection

  • The target service (WhatsApp, Telegram, etc.) rejects the number — this is outside our control
  • Geographic restrictions imposed by the target service

3.4 Wallet Funding

  • Payment processor fees (Paystack charges) are non-refundable
  • Wallet credits cannot be withdrawn as cash — they can only be used for verifications

4. Number Rental Refunds

4.1 Early Release

If you release a rented number before expiration:

  • Refund: 50% of the unused time value
  • Minimum usage: 1 hour must have elapsed
  • Formula: (Unused hours ÷ Total hours) × 50% × Cost

4.2 Example

Scenario: You rent a number for 7 days ($5.00) but release after 2 days.
Used: 2 days (48 hours)  |  Unused: 5 days (120 hours)
Refund: (120 ÷ 168) × 50% × $5.00 = $1.79

4.3 No Refund for Rentals

  • Expired rentals (full duration used)
  • Rentals with less than 1 hour remaining
  • Rentals where an SMS was received (service was used)

5. Unused Credits

  • Credits remain in your wallet indefinitely — no expiration
  • Credits cannot be withdrawn as cash
  • If the service is permanently discontinued, we will provide a cash refund process

6. Dispute Resolution

6.1 Refund Disputes

If you believe you are entitled to a refund that was not issued:

  1. Email support@vrenum.app with subject "Refund Request — [Verification ID]"
  2. Include your verification ID and a description of the issue
  3. We will review and respond within 24–48 hours

6.2 Chargeback Policy

Initiating a chargeback without contacting us first may result in immediate account suspension and forfeiture of remaining credits. Always contact support first — we resolve disputes fairly and quickly.

7. Fraud Prevention

Patterns of refund abuse — including false non-delivery claims, cancelling after receiving codes, or creating multiple accounts to exploit refunds — will result in account suspension and may be reported to relevant authorities.

8. Contact

Email: support@vrenum.app
Response time: Within 24–48 hours
